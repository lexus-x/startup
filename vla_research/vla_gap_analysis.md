# Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Model Research Gap Analysis

## Objective
Identify 10+ unsolved gaps in small-scale VLA models (<120M params) for real-time 6-DoF robotic arm control, focusing on modular solutions that keep base weights frozen.

## Key VLA Papers & Surveys to Analyze

### Foundational VLA Works:
1. RT-2 (Robotics Transformer 2) - Google DeepMind
2. OpenVLA - Stanford/UC Berkeley
3. Octo - UC Berkeley
4. Pi0 - Physical Intelligence
5. Diffusion Policy - Columbia
6. ACT (Action Chunking with Transformers) - MIT
7. BridgeData V2 - UC Berkeley

### Survey Papers:
1. "A Survey on Vision-Language-Action Models" (2024)
2. "Embodied AI: A Survey" (2024)
3. "Robotic Manipulation with Vision-Language Models" (2024)
